Designed for zero friction.

From clean installation to modular activation in three straightforward steps.

1 Core APK

Grant Root & Register

Open Hollow, grant superuser access (`su`) so the root vault can be initialized, and create your account right inside the app.

2 Verification

Account Review

Your registration enters review to maintain a trusted community. As soon as your account is approved, the app transitions automatically.

3 One-Tap Sync

Install Modules on Demand

Approved modules appear directly on your dashboard. Verify their SHA-256 and Ed25519 signatures and mount them with a single tap.

Check Device Approval Status

Waiting for your account review to complete? Enter your username or the Hardware Verification ID (HLW-…) from the app to check your current approval state.

Your Hollow app also checks approval status automatically every 5 seconds while on the Account Under Review screen.
