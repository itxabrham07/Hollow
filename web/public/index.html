Four Modular Privacy Layers

Each capability is delivered as an independently signed module that mounts only when authorized for your device.

folder_supervised

Storage Isolation Workspace

Scoped File & Media Boundary

Prevents applications from scanning your personal DCIM photos, documents, and root directories. Each isolated app operates inside a dedicated workspace volume while keeping your personal files private.

fingerprint_off

Device Identity & Telemetry Shield

Anti-Tracking & Identifier Hygiene

Protects against cross-app hardware fingerprinting by providing clean, per-application device profiles for Android ID, advertising identifiers, sensor telemetry, and WebView fingerprinting vectors.

visibility_lock

App Package Visibility Control

Package Enumeration Protection

Stops intrusive SDKs from inventorying the other applications installed on your phone. Manage which apps are visible in package queries and customize workspace launcher presentation.

vpn_lock

Per-App Encrypted Network Routing

Scoped Proxy & Tunneling

Assign dedicated SOCKS5, HTTP, or V2Ray tunnels on a per-application basis with automatic timezone alignment and DNS leak prevention so your network metadata stays compartmentalized.

How Device Onboarding Works

A clean, three-step activation flow designed for zero clutter.

Step 1

Grant Root & Register

Install the lightweight Hollow Core APK, grant superuser permission when prompted, and create your account or sign in.

Step 2

Administrator Approval

Your device enters the clean Waiting for Approval screen while the administrator verifies and approves your device in the Admin Console.

Step 3

Automatic Module Sync

Once approved, Hollow automatically downloads, verifies (Ed25519 + SHA-256), and mounts your authorized privacy modules onto your Dashboard.
